# SUFS Purchasing Guide Reference (2025-2026)

This file summarizes eligible expense categories for PEP and FES-UA scholarships.
Used by the receipt parser to correctly categorize line items.

---

## Instructional Materials

### At-Home Classroom Furnishings
Desk, chair, whiteboard, bulletin board, bookshelf, storage/organizers, maps/globes/atlases,
educational posters, indoor projector (2-year limit), television up to 55" (2-year limit),
picnic table, architect/drafting table, rugs/foam tiles, CD/DVD/music players, timers/clocks.

### Books
Textbooks, workbooks, and other books. No spending cap. Two copies permitted.

### Digital Materials
CDs, DVDs, digital documents, eBook/audiobook subscriptions, productivity/educational apps.

### Educational Software & Subscriptions
Word processing software, Microsoft Office, Adobe suite, video/music editing software,
magazine/newspaper subscriptions, streaming services (video, music), graphics/photo editing software.
Note: social media (except LinkedIn Learning) and in-game purchases/credits are NOT eligible.

### Educational Subscription Kits
Subscription kits curated by a kit company (e.g. science kits, craft kits).

### Electives
**Courses:** Art, music, cooking, drama, foreign language, photography/videography, sewing,
woodworking, gardening, programming/digital production, mechanical, animal husbandry, enrichment
classes, club fees (Scouts, 4H, FFA).
**Supplies:** Art supplies (paint, pencils, canvas, clay), cooking supplies (utensils, measuring cups),
sewing supplies (thread, fabric, needles), woodworking supplies (wood, nails, sandpaper),
gardening (soil, seeds, seedlings, small plants), photography (batteries).
**Equipment (2-year limit):** Cameras, tripods, microphones, sewing machines, easels, kilns,
power tools, standing mixers, blenders.

### Field Trips & Other Activities
Aquarium, zoo, museum admission/memberships; state/national park admission; ticketed events
(plays, musicals, orchestral performances); single-admission leisure events under $75 (bowling,
roller skating, ice skating, trampoline parks including equipment rental).
Florida theme parks up to $299/year per student: Disney (Magic Kingdom, Epcot, Hollywood Studios,
Animal Kingdom), Universal (Universal Studios, Islands of Adventure, Epic Universe), SeaWorld,
Busch Gardens, LEGOLAND Florida, Peppa Pig Theme Park, Gatorland, Dinosaur World.

### Internet Resources
Home internet service, mobile hotspots, modem/router rental billed with internet service.
Age-appropriate streaming services.

### Lab Fees & Materials
Science, math, and computer lab fees and equipment. Lab safety equipment.

### Learning Manipulatives & Creative Play Items
Board games, card games, blocks, LEGO sets, puzzles, chess/checker sets, dominoes,
dolls and accessories, stuffed animals, marbles, dress-up clothing, play kitchen sets, water tables.

### Musical Instruments & Equipment
Musical instruments, maintenance, tuning, insurance, repair, service agreements.

### Physical Education (P.E.)
**Lessons/Fees:** Dance lessons, sports lessons (individual or group), gym/fitness memberships
(student only), team fees (registration, equipment rental, competition), stand-alone athletic events
(race registrations, endurance events, Special Olympics).
**Supplies:** Sports balls, racquets, dance supplies, specialized/sport-specific footwear,
sports uniforms and required accessories, swim goggles/snorkels/fins/masks.
**Equipment (2-year limit):** Basketball hoops/goals/nets, stationary bike, treadmill/walking pad,
weight bench, home playset/swing set, ping-pong table, foosball table, air hockey, pool table,
two/three-wheeled bicycles, kayak, canoe, surfboard, stand-up paddleboard, boogie board,
skim board, skateboard, individual-sized trampoline, Nintendo Wii or movement-based platforms.
**Not eligible:** Motorized equipment/vehicles, in-ground/above-ground pools, large trampolines,
bounce houses, inflatable slides, non-sport-specific footwear.

### School Supplies
Backpacks/bookbags, notebooks, folders, binders, paper, pencils, pens, crayons, markers,
colored pencils, calculators, scissors, tape, paperclips, staplers, adhesives, rulers, compasses,
protractors, abacus, lunchboxes/bento boxes, planners/organizers, printer ink, wax filament,
prescription eyeglasses (1/year).

### Sensory Materials
Balance ball, sensory swings, sensory tables, sensory lighting (lava lamps, bubble tubes),
fidget spinners and sensory manipulatives, weighted blankets/bundles, therapy chairs,
specialized comfort furniture, air walkers.

## Curriculum & Curriculum Materials
Full curriculum packages and curriculum materials (textbooks, workbooks, teacher guides, etc.)

## Tuition & Fees
Private school tuition, home education instructional programs, postsecondary tuition,
dual enrollment, pre-apprenticeship programs, private tutoring (full-time programs),
approved online/virtual providers.

Standardized tests, assessments, evaluation fees.

## FES-UA Only Categories

### Digital Devices (UA only — 2-year limit)
Laptop, desktop computer, tablet, e-Reader, cellular phone, smart watch, drone,
gaming system/console (must come with educational software).

### Digital Periphery Devices (UA only — 2-year limit for some)
Keyboard, mouse/touchpad/trackball, computer monitor, printer (including 3D printers),
speakers, headsets, external hard drive/flash drive, scanner, digital pencil,
VR/AR headsets, Wi-Fi router/modem/extender, case and screen protector.

### Assistive Technology Devices (UA only)
Hearing aids, prescription eyeglasses, Braille displays/embossers, augmentative communication
devices, screen readers/magnifiers, FM systems, speech/voice recognition programs,
text-to-speech synthesizers, smart boards, vibroacoustic therapy devices.

### Specialized Services (UA only)
Licensed ABA therapy, Speech therapy, Occupational therapy, Physical therapy.

### Horse Therapy (UA only)
Therapeutic horseback riding for eligible students.

### Music and Art Therapy (UA only)
Music therapy and art therapy services provided by licensed therapists.

### Specialized Summer/After-School Programs (UA only)
Fees for specialized summer or after-school education programs.

### Transition Services (UA only)
Job coaching and transition services for post-secondary preparation.

### College Savings Contributions (UA only)
Florida Prepaid (529) and Florida 529 College Savings Program contributions.
