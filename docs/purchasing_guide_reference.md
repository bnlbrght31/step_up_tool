# SUFS Purchasing Guide Reference (2026-2027)

This file summarizes eligible expense categories for PEP and FES-UA scholarships.
Used by the receipt parser to correctly categorize line items, and by the Amazon
scanner to screen eligibility.

Sources:
- FES-UA Purchasing Guidelines 2026-2027 (UCF/FCSUA, revised July 1, 2026) — `docs/sources/fesua-purchasing-guide-2026-27.pdf`
- PEP Purchasing Guide 2026-27 (Step Up For Students, last modified Aug 4, 2026) — web-only,
  https://www.stepupforstudents.org/handbook-web/pep-purchasing-guide/

**The two programs differ.** Items marked `UA only` are eligible for FES-UA but
explicitly prohibited under PEP. Items marked `PEP prohibited` must never be
categorized as eligible for a PEP student.

---

## Instructional Materials

### At-Home Classroom Furnishings
Desk, chair, whiteboard, bulletin board, bookshelf, storage/organizers, maps/globes/atlases,
educational posters/visual supports, indoor projector, television (PEP: up to 55"; over 55"
needs pre-auth), picnic table, architect/drafting table, rugs/foam tiles, standing or
drop-down screens, CD/DVD/music players, timers/clocks.
UA also allows other furniture that supports student learning needs.
*Frequency:* once every 2 years for television, architect/drafting table, picnic table, projector.
*Prohibited:* outdoor televisions, inflatable outdoor theatres.

### Books
Textbooks, workbooks, and other books. No spending cap, no frequency limit. Two copies permitted.
*Prohibited:* books promoting violence or criminal behavior, or posing a threat to the student.

### Digital Materials
CDs, DVDs, digital documents, eBook/audiobook subscriptions, productivity/behavioral/educational apps.
Multi-user purchases are prorated to the student's share.

### Educational Software & Subscriptions
Single-user/student pricing only; multi-user access is prorated to the student's cost.
Eligible: word processing software, Microsoft Office, Adobe, video/music editing software,
LexisNexis, QuickBooks, graphics/photo editing software, magazine/newspaper subscriptions,
streaming services (video, TV, online, music).
*Not eligible:* in-game purchases/credits, live television, social media (except LinkedIn
Learning for Students), family/multi-user subscriptions billed without proration.
*Prohibited:* gaming subscription services.

> NOTE: The 2026-27 PEP web guide lists the four "not eligible" items above inside its
> "eligible expenses" bullet list, with no prohibited-items heading — almost certainly a
> page-authoring error, since "social media (except LinkedIn Learning)" only parses as an
> exclusion and these were explicitly ineligible in 2025-26. Treated as ineligible here.
> Re-check against the PEP page if an item in this group is ever denied or approved.

### Educational Subscription Kits
Subscription kits curated and mailed by a kit company (science kits, craft kits, etc.).
*Prohibited:* kits not age- and size-appropriate for the student.

### Electives
**Courses:** animal husbandry, art, club fees (Scouts, 4-H, FFA), culinary/cooking, drama,
foreign language, gardening/horticultural, mechanical training, music,
photography/videography, programming/digital production, sewing, woodworking/building.

**Course supplies:** animal husbandry (bedding, grooming, cleaning supplies); art (paint,
pencils, canvases, paper, clay, glue); cooking (measuring cups, utensils, mixing bowls,
subscription kits); drama (costumes and accessories, except costume and fine jewelry);
gardening (soil, trellises, drip irrigation, fertilizer, seeds, bulbs, seedlings, potted herbs,
fruit/vegetable plants, pollinator plants, plants in containers up to one gallon; fruit
trees/shrubs up to seven-gallon pots); mechanical (screws, wires, lubricants, solder);
photography/video (batteries, tripods, lenses, and — UA only — microphones); safety supplies
(disposable gloves, masks, earplugs); sewing (thread, fabric, needles, patterns, bobbins);
postsecondary and career education (tape, labels); woodworking (wood, nails, glue, sandpaper, stain).

**Course equipment (2-year frequency limit):** animal husbandry (heating lamps, incubators,
feeders, grooming); art (easels, ceramics kiln); cooking (stand mixers, blenders, dehydrators);
gardening (raised beds, compost tumblers, hydroponic garden kits); mechanical (power tools,
engine models); photography/video (cameras, lighting kits); programming/digital production
(monitors — UA only); safety supplies (non-disposable goggles, gloves, respirators); sewing
(sewing machine, mannequins, rotary cutters); postsecondary/career education (toolboxes,
specialized trade tools); woodworking (workbenches, clamps, drills, sanders).

*Prohibited:* general household expense or improvement supplies (large irrigation systems,
large appliances, chicken coops); gas or electric-powered gardening tools; performance costumes (PEP).

### Field Trips & Other Activities
Student admission only; family packages are prorated to the student's share. Tickets must come
from the venue or an authorized third-party seller.
Eligible: cultural programming events/facilities, aquarium/zoo/museum admission and memberships,
programs or events at museums/zoos/aquariums, museum or library memberships, state/national park
admission, ticketed events (plays, musicals, orchestral performances), school field trip fees paid
to an eligible private school or home education program, single-admission leisure events under $75
(bowling, roller skating, ice skating, trampoline parks including equipment rental).

**Florida theme parks — up to $299 before tax, one reimbursement per student for the school year
(July 1, 2026 – July 31, 2027).** Must be submitted alone, not bundled with other items. Eligible
parks: Walt Disney World (Magic Kingdom, Epcot, Hollywood Studios, Animal Kingdom), Universal
Orlando (Universal Studios Florida, Islands of Adventure, Epic Universe), SeaWorld Orlando, Busch
Gardens Tampa Bay, LEGOLAND Florida Resort, Peppa Pig Theme Park, Gatorland, Dinosaur World.
*Prohibited:* parking, food and beverage packages, photographs, souvenirs, premium access,
special event/seasonal/after-hours tickets, water parks, out-of-state parks, secondary-market
or resale tickets, group admissions or rentals.

### Internet Resources
In-home internet service and mobile hotspots at the student's Florida residential address.
Modem/router rental billed with monthly internet service. Age-appropriate streaming services.
Internet service is not prorated for other family members.
*Prohibited:* business, mobile, and Starlink RV internet services.

### Lab Fees & Materials
Science, math, and computer lab fees and equipment; lab safety equipment.
*Prohibited:* lab equipment posing a threat or promoting violence or criminal behavior.

### Learning Manipulatives & Creative Play Items
Board/card games, blocks, chess and checker sets, dolls and accessories, dominoes, marbles,
dress-up clothing and accessories (except costume and fine jewelry), LEGO sets, play kitchen sets,
puzzles, stuffed animals, water tables.
*Prohibited:* games or puzzles posing a threat or promoting violence or criminal behavior.

### Musical Instruments & Equipment
Musical instruments, musical equipment rentals, maintenance, tuning, insurance, repair,
service agreements.

### Physical Education (P.E.)
Single-person items for the student, or shared-use equipment where the student needs an
accompanying adult for safe use.
**Lessons/fees:** dance lessons, sports lessons (individual or group), gym/fitness memberships and
class passes (student only, including on-demand fitness classes), team fees (registration,
equipment rental, competition), stand-alone athletic event fees (race registrations, endurance
events, Special Olympics), digital or video-based sport instruction (PEP: pre-auth required).
**Supplies:** sports balls, racquets, basketball hoops/goals, soccer goals, tennis/volleyball nets,
sports supplies rentals (helmets, bats, lacrosse sticks, gloves, shoulder/knee pads), dance supplies
including footwear, specialized or sport-specific footwear, sports uniforms and required accessories,
swim goggles/kickboards/snorkels/masks/fins, safety equipment.
**Equipment (2-year frequency limit):** stationary bike, treadmill/walking pad, weight bench, home
playset/swing set (including rubber mulch or safety materials), table sports (ping-pong, pool,
foosball, air hockey, basketball shooting games), two- or three-wheeled bicycles, kayak, canoe,
surfboard, stand-up paddleboard, boogie board, skim board, skateboard, individual-sized trampoline,
Nintendo Wii or similar movement-based platforms.
*Prohibited:* motorized equipment (treadmills/walking pads are eligible), motorized scooters or
ride-ons including golf carts, motorized vehicles (two-, three-, four-wheeled), motorized watercraft
and sailboats, in-ground/above-ground pools and Jacuzzis, trampolines larger than individual-sized,
bounce houses, inflatable slides, non-sport-specific footwear, group rentals, out-of-state activities
that are not pre-authorized.

### School Supplies
Backpacks/bookbags/tote bags/messenger bags, notebooks, folders, binders, paper, writing utensils
(pencils, pens, crayons, colored pencils, markers), calculators, math tools (abacus, compass,
protractor, ruler; PEP also analog/digital watches), scissors, tape, paperclips, staplers, adhesives,
lunchboxes/bento boxes, planners/organizers, printer ink, wax filament (PEP), prescription eyeglasses.
*Frequency:* prescription eyeglasses once per year.

### Sensory Materials
Balance ball, manipulatives and hands-on materials, sensory swings, sensory tables, sensory lighting
(lava lamps, bubble tubes), sensory manipulatives (fidget spinners, Boink toys), sensory bundles
(travel sensory kit, calming weighted bundle), specialized furniture/comfort chairs (beanbag chairs,
Nugget couches).
UA also includes: air walker, therapy chair, and OT/PT/speech-language therapy augmentation.

## Curriculum & Curriculum Materials
Complete courses of study for a content area or grade level, including required supplemental
materials, teacher manuals, and associated online instruction. Includes postsecondary programs
aimed at career readiness or trade certification.
PEP: self-paced, pre-recorded online courses belong here; live online instruction goes under
Tuition & Fees at an Approved Online or Virtual Provider. PEP funds may not pay for full-time
online instruction.
*Pre-auth required* for curriculum that is not publicly available.

---

## Tuition & Fees
Eligible private school, PEP hybrid private school (PEP), home education instructional programs,
eligible postsecondary institutions, dual enrollment, FLDOE-approved pre-apprenticeship programs,
full-time private tutoring programs, approved online/virtual providers, Florida Virtual School as
a private-pay student.

**Eligible school fees:** academic services, activity (choir, band), application, athletics, book,
club, enrollment, graduation, registration, security, technology, test, uniform (basic pieces from
a school-required vendor).
*Prohibited fees:* annual funds, before/after-school care, scholarship management costs, food fees
(breakfast, lunch), fundraising/donation/giving/volunteer waiver fees, optional fees, shoes and
accessories, nursery or daycare fees.

---

## Testing & Assessment Fees
Nationally and state standardized norm-referenced assessments, Advanced Placement (AP) exams,
industry certification exams, assessments related to postsecondary education, career and interest
assessments, initial or re-evaluation assessments (placement exams, mid-year evaluations).
UA also includes transition assessments.
*Pre-auth required* (UA) for testing or exams not listed in the guide.

---

## Contracted Services Provided by a Public School or School District
Courses, programming (after-school, extracurricular, supplemental, summer school), or services
purchased from a public school, charter school, school district, or FLVS on a private-pay basis.
Parent pays and requests reimbursement, or the district is paid directly as a provider.

---

## Part-Time Tutoring & Choice Navigator Services
Part-time tutoring by a credentialed provider, tutoring program fees, and Choice Navigator
consultation fees.

## FES-UA Only Categories

These are eligible for FES-UA and **prohibited under PEP** — see Appendix A of the PEP guide,
which lists digital devices and digital periphery devices explicitly as prohibited items.

### Digital Devices (UA only — 2-year frequency limit; PEP prohibited)
Laptop, desktop computer, tablet, e-Reader, cellular phone, smart watch, drone,
gaming system/console purchased with accompanying educational software.
PEP note: gaming systems are prohibited except Nintendo Wii or similar movement-based platforms,
which belong under Physical Education.

### Digital Periphery Devices (UA only; PEP prohibited)
Keyboard, mouse/touchpad/trackball, computer monitor, printers (regular, 3D, sublimation, photo),
speakers, headsets/headphones/earbuds, external hard drive/flash drive, scanner, digital pencil,
virtual and augmented reality headsets/goggles, wands and sticks, Wi-Fi routers/modems/extenders,
case and screen protector, digital periphery bundles.
No frequency-of-purchase limit as of 2026-27.
PEP note: router/modem rental billed with monthly internet service is eligible under Internet Resources.

### Assistive Technology Devices (UA only — 1-year frequency limit)
Hearing aids, prescription eyeglasses, Braille displays/embossers, augmentative communication
devices, screen readers/magnifiers/enlargers, FM systems, interactive metronome, light signaler
alerts, Proloquo2Go, reading tools, sip-and-puff systems, smart boards, speech/voice recognition
programs, student response systems, switches, talking and large-print word processors,
text-to-speech/speech synthesizers, TTY/TTD conversion modems, vibroacoustic therapy devices.
*Pre-auth required* for assistive devices not listed.

### Training On & Maintenance Agreements For Eligible Devices (UA only)
Training for parents/guardians or students on using eligible devices; manufacturer or vendor
maintenance agreements and warranties for devices purchased with FES-UA funds.

### Specialized Services (UA only)
Licensed ABA therapy, speech-language therapy, occupational therapy, physical therapy, listening
and spoken language therapy, psychotherapy/counseling by a licensed psychologist, school
psychologist, mental health counselor, marriage and family therapist, or clinical social worker,
and vision therapy by a licensed optometrist.

### Horse Therapy (UA only)
Services at a center that is a member of the Professional Association of Therapeutic Horsemanship
International (PATH). Certification must belong to the center, not an individual.

### Music and Art Therapy (UA only)
Therapists certified by the Certification Board for Music Therapists or credentialed by the
Art Therapy Credentials Board.

### Specialized Summer Education Program (UA only)
Fees for specialized summer education programs, or other programs during standard school breaks,
developed for students with special or unique needs. Published fees must be provided to the SFO;
proof of attendance required. Overnight or day educational camps may be permitted with pre-auth.

### Specialized After-School Education Program (UA only)
Fees for specialized after-school education programs. Proof of attendance required. Camp lodging
and food are not covered. Full or half day programs permitted for students age 14 and up.

### Transition Services & Job Coaches (UA only)
Transition-to-work programs, and job coaching for students ages 12 through 22: career and interest
assessments, self-advocacy training, employability/soft skills, job development and training,
interview support, onsite coaching, follow-along support, benefits planning, advocacy services.

### College Savings Contributions (UA only)
Stanley G. Tate Florida Prepaid College Program (Prepaid 529) and Florida 529 College Savings
Program (Investment 529, "Family Empowerment Plan") contributions.

### Annual Home Education Evaluation Fees (UA only)
Fees for the annual evaluation of a home education student's progress by a state-certified teacher.

## Prohibited & Ineligible Items

Never categorize these as eligible. SUFS will not approve pre-authorization for prohibited items.

**Always prohibited (both programs):**
- Activities occurring outside Florida that are not pre-authorized
- Amusement/theme parks not on the eligible list, water parks, out-of-state theme parks
- Any item posing a threat to the student or others, or promoting violence or criminal behavior
- Any item where the student does not meet the manufacturer's minimum age requirement
- Arcade games or visits; bouncy houses; inflatable slides
- Business, mobile, and Starlink RV internet services
- Commercial-grade tools and equipment
- Costs of accommodations (lodging, meals) and transportation (gas, mileage, public transit)
- Food and beverage
- Gaming subscription services
- Gas or electric-powered gardening tools
- Group admissions or rentals
- Guns, blades, and knives (except scissors, math tools, or elective equipment)
- Household items and home improvement, other than cooking supplies or At-Home Classroom
  Furnishings list items
- Housing or food for live animals; live animals
- In-ground or above-ground swimming pools and Jacuzzis
- Live mature or ornamental plants over one gallon (fruit trees/shrubs up to seven gallons allowed);
  ornamental plants (shrubs, landscaping, houseplants)
- Meet and greet events
- Medical items — mobility aids, contact lenses, non-prescription eyeglasses, prescription
  sunglasses (one pair of prescription eyeglasses per year is allowed)
- Motorized exercise equipment (treadmills and walking pads are eligible), motorized scooters or
  ride-ons including golf carts, motorized vehicles, motorized watercraft and sailboats
- Outdoor televisions and inflatable outdoor theatres
- Photographs, souvenirs, premium access, and special event tickets outside basic admission
- Secondary market or resale tickets; sporting event tickets
- Smart and augmented reality prescription glasses
- Toy weapons — toy arrows, Nerf guns, airsoft guns, pellet and BB guns, water guns, toy pistols,
  rifles, shotguns, toy swords, knives or daggers, toy axes or hatchets, toy spears, nunchucks,
  throwing stars, toy grenades, toy tactical vests paired with toy weapons, slingshots, dart guns
- Trampolines other than individual exercise trampolines
- Watercraft other than kayaks, canoes, surfboards, boogie boards, skim boards, stand-up paddleboards

**PEP only — additionally prohibited:**
- All digital devices (cell phones, desktops, laptops including Chromebook/Surface, e-Readers,
  smart watches) and digital periphery devices (monitors, keyboards, mice, printers including 3D,
  speakers, headsets, external drives, scanners, digital pencils, VR/AR headsets, Wi-Fi routers)
- Gaming systems and consoles including Nintendo Switch (Nintendo Wii and similar movement-based
  platforms remain eligible under P.E.)
- Full-time online instruction
- Concerts; performance costumes; pet beds and cushions
- Televisions over 55"
- Footwear that is not specialized or sport-specific
- Single-admission leisure events over $75, including equipment rental

**UA only — additionally prohibited:**
- Cash payments to private sellers
- Medical services (doctors' visits, examinations, medical devices) outside the legislated
  Specialized Services
- Duplicate billing — items already paid by insurance, an HSA, or a School Readiness program

## Items Requiring Pre-Authorization

- Anything not listed as eligible in the guides
- Curriculum that is not publicly available
- Items falling outside frequency-of-purchase limits (Digital Devices, P.E., Electives,
  At-Home Classroom Furnishings)
- P.E. activities occurring outside Florida
- Non-school-affiliated field trips outside Florida but within the United States
- Televisions over 55 inches (UA)
- Assistive devices not enumerated in the guide (UA)
- Standardized testing or exams not listed (UA)
- Overnight or day educational camps, with documentation of educational benefit (UA)
- Digital or video-based sport instruction (PEP)

SFO-approved military families living outside Florida are exempt from the out-of-state
pre-authorization requirements. Field trips outside the United States are never eligible.
