First-time setup — launch Chrome for the agent
  1. Launch Chrome with remote debugging (Terminal): /Applications/Google\ Chrome.app/Contents/MacOS/Google\ Chrome --remote-debugging-port=9222 --user-data-dir="$HOME/.sufs-agent-chrome"
  2. Log in to Step Up for Students in that window and open your reimbursement request
  3. Upload your receipt on the SUFS site, then drop it in above
  4. Review the extracted items and click Fill Form
  5. The agent fills the form — you review and submit
