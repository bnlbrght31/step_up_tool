SUFS Reimbursement Agent

Upload a receipt PDF and the agent will fill the form for you.

  1. Launch Chrome with remote debugging (Terminal):
    /Applications/Google\ Chrome.app/Contents/MacOS/Google\ Chrome --remote-debugging-port=9222 --user-data-dir="$HOME/.sufs-agent-chrome"
  2. Log in to Step Up for Students in that Chrome window and open your reimbursement request
  3. Upload your receipt on the SUFS site, then upload it below
  4. Review extracted items and click Fill Form
  5. The agent fills the form — you submit
